## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an evocative, high-end editorial presence tailored for an emerging marketing strategist and brand consultant operating at the intersection of corporate finance, consulting, and luxury hospitality. It merges the poise of legacy luxury publishing with the structured precision of modern executive portfolio design.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Sophisticated & Authoritative:** Instills immediate trust, signaling business acumen, strategic depth, and high corporate standards.
- **Warm & Cultivated:** Draws inspiration from sunlit African luxury lodges, earthy sands, and natural tactile elegance, avoiding cold corporate sterility.
- **Editorial & Modern:** Evokes the feeling of reading a bespoke monograph or high-end industry journal with generous whitespace, deliberate typography contrasts, and structured, quiet boundaries.

### Design Movement
**Modern Editorial Minimalism with Warm Tactile Accents.** The system relies on stark typographic scale, disciplined grid geometry, understated micro-borders, and rich canvas-like surfaces rather than heavy skeuomorphism or overt decorative noise.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classic savannas, starlit nights, and artisanal stationery. It anchors on deep nocturnal indigo, contrasted against sand-champagne accents and warm cream foundational surfaces.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0F172A` - Midnight Slate):** Used for commanding typography, primary buttons, major section headers, and high-impact structural panels.
- **Secondary (`#C59B5F` - Polished Champagne Sand):** Acts as the signature warm metallic accent. Deployed across fine dividing lines, interactive active states, badges, decorative metrics, and high-prestige tags.
- **Tertiary (`#78350F` - Deep Amber Umber):** Serves as an anchoring contrast point for specialized contextual tags, sub-headings, or editorial callouts.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B` - Ink Navy):** The primary reading body text color, offering softer contrast than absolute black on cream.
- **Canvas & Backgrounds:**
  - Base canvas: `#FAFAF9` (Stone Cream), producing a warm, paper-like tactile feel.
  - Surface Container / Card Base: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Studio White) for crisp content separation.
  - Subtle Surface Alt: `#F5F3EF` (Woven Linen) for alternating section backdrops or secondary card states.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the classical literary poise of **EB Garamond** with the clean, contemporary legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Roles & Execution
- **Headlines & Editorial Callouts:** Handled exclusively by `EB Garamond`. Display sizes should embrace slight tracking contraction and soft italicized variations for storytelling emphasis (e.g., highlighting roles, locations like *Victoria Falls*, or strategic philosophies).
- **Body & Data:** Powered by `Plus Jakarta Sans` at regular 400 weight with generous line-heights, ensuring friction-free scanability for long-form case studies, deliverables, and credential timelines.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Always cast in uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans` with expanded letter spacing (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to create distinct, publication-grade metadata markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors high-end editorial print layouts, utilizing a structured 12-column grid constrained within a maximum page width of `1280px` for desktop viewports.

### Grid Rhythm & Adapters
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns with `2.5rem` gutters and outer margins set to `margin-desktop` (`5rem`). Allows deliberate asymmetrical alignments, such as an off-center 4-column profile narrative set adjacent to an 8-column case study stream.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, transitioning margins to `2.5rem` and gutters to `1.5rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding and `1rem` column gutters. All dual-column cards collapse to single stacked units.

### Spacing Principles
Component density remains intentionally open and deliberate. Whitespace acts as a luxury asset, separating major career milestones, client showcase blocks, and reflective excerpts with ample breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids high-blur drop shadows and heavy multi-layered z-index shifts, relying instead on architectural layering and hairline boundary treatments.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Flat Base Canvas:** `#FAFAF9` grounding the overall experience.
2. **Elevated Content Modules:** Pure white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) framed by fine, low-contrast borders: `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07)` or accented by champagne hairline borders: `1px solid rgba(197, 155, 95, 0.3)`.
3. **Ambient Hover Float:** When a card or interactive case study is engaged, it gains a subtle upward translate (`-2px` to `-4px`) paired with a whisper-light tinted ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
4. **Dividers & Structural Rules:** Hairline horizontal rules (`1px solid #E7E5E4`) separate career milestones, keeping the layout grounded like a bespoke broadsheet.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a curated, softened rectangular geometry (`Soft` - level 1).

### Geometrics
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Cards):** Built using an intentional, tailored corner radius of `4px` (`0.25rem`), preserving an architectural, high-end stationery aesthetic.
- **Large Panels & Media Containers:** Utilize `rounded-lg` (`8px` / `0.5rem`) to frame imagery smoothly without rounding away the crisp modern edge.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted strictly to micro-tags, metric pills, and category chips (`rounded-full`) to contrast against rectilinear card structures.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Dark navy (`#0F172A`) solid background, pure white text, 4px corner radius, standard padding of `0.75rem 1.75rem`. On hover, micro-shifts tone to slate navy (`#1E293B`) with a smooth 150ms transition.
- **Secondary (The Champagne Stroke):** Transparent background, `1px solid #C59B5F` border, `#0F172A` text. On hover, background fills with `rgba(197, 155, 95, 0.08)`.
- **Text Link Button:** Bold label font with an active underline offset by 4px, rendered in `#C59B5F` or `#0F172A`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Experience Tags & Specializations:** Rendered as pill-shaped or soft-rounded components using uppercase `label-sm` tracking. Background set to soft sand tint (`rgba(197, 155, 95, 0.12)`), text rendered in deep amber umber (`#78350F`) or midnight slate (`#0F172A`).
- **Institutional Badges:** (e.g., "Baker Tilly Central Africa", "Victoria Falls Safari Lodge"): Feature an off-white `#F5F3EF` background, sharp 4px corners, and a light `#E7E5E4` perimeter line.

### Cards & Case Study Showcases
- White containers on the cream canvas with 4px border radius.
- Standard inner padding: `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) on desktop; `space-md` (`1rem`) on mobile.
- Header region within cards includes a category label in champagne gold, an `EB Garamond` title, and an integrated year/client marker.
- Borders use `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`. On interactive cards, hover triggers a champagne border transition: `border-color: #C59B5F`.

### Lists & Timeline Components
- Used for curriculum vitae milestones, education, and brand achievements.
- Employs a border-left hairline accent (`1px solid #E2C392`) connecting entries vertically.
- Milestones feature an offset year/tenure in `label-md` tracking, paired with an `EB Garamond` role title and brief contextual accomplishments in `body-md`.

### Form & Input Fields
- Understated, architectural text boxes with soft cream-white fills (`#FFFFFF`).
- Resting border: `1px solid #D6D3D1`.
- Focused state: `1px solid #0F172A` with an ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #0F172A`.
- Labels float top-aligned in `label-sm` uppercase.

### Media & Quote Callouts
- Editorial pull quotes: Centered or indented, set in `headline-sm` italic `EB Garamond`, bracketed top and bottom by 40px centered champagne horizontal bars (`2px solid #C59B5F`).
